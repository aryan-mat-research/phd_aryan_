CVD Growth and Characterization of 2D Semiconductors

Experimental growth and characterization of transition-metal dichalcogenides, with attention to how growth conditions relate to morphology and structural characteristics.

Overview

Research question

How do growth temperature, dwell time, and precursor deposition conditions influence the nucleation, lateral growth, and triangular island morphology of two-dimensional transition-metal dichalcogenides synthesized by chemical vapor deposition?

Methods

Materials, methods & workflow

CVD growth and characterization using Raman spectroscopy, AFM, FESEM, and XPS. Materials studied include MoS₂, MoSe₂, WSe₂, WS₂, and MoSe₂–WSe₂ heterostructures.
