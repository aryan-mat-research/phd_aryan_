DFT and AIMD Studies of MoO₃ Structural Disorder

First-principles modeling of molybdenum trioxide to investigate structural relaxation, local Mo–O coordination, and the evolution of atomic environments in crystalline and potentially disordered configurations.

Density Functional Theory Ab Initio Molecular Dynamics Quantum ESPRESSO PBE–PAW Structural Analysis

Research objective

Establish a first-principles workflow for relaxing MoO₃ structures and examining local atomic environments. The broader aim is to determine which structural descriptors can distinguish crystalline order from thermally induced disorder after the relevant trajectories and final configurations have been validated.

Central questions

  • How do local Mo–O distances and coordination environments change?
  • How does the pair distribution function evolve with structural disorder?
  • Which temperature and energy signatures can be extracted from the AIMD trajectory?
  • Are final structures sufficiently equilibrated and statistically representative?

Quantifying local order and disorder

Radial distribution function, g(r)

Compare the first-neighbour peak position, peak width, and longer-range oscillations between the crystalline reference and validated disordered structures.

g(r) describes the radial probability of finding a neighbour at distance r relative to an appropriate reference density.

Report: frame range, bin width, normalization, periodic-boundary handling, and number of sampled frames.

Figure 4B · Mo–O bond lengths
Histogram or probability density versus distance (Å).
Show crystalline and disordered datasets separately.

Bond-length distribution

Quantify the distribution of local Mo–O distances and identify broadening or multiple local environments.

Report: neighbour cutoff or bond definition, mean, median, standard deviation, and sampled atom count.

Local coordination

Measure the distribution of oxygen neighbours around Mo atoms and assess whether local environments become more heterogeneous.

Density / structure factor

Density requires a reliable volume and composition. A structure factor requires a documented calculation from the structure or trajectory. These should not be inferred from an image alone.

Report: volume convention, normalization, reciprocal-space units, and averaging procedure.

Interpretation framework

  • Short-range order: assess nearest-neighbour peak positions and widths in g(r).
  • Local geometry: compare Mo–O distance and bond-angle distributions.
  • Coordination: evaluate the fraction of Mo sites in each coordination environment under a consistent cutoff.
  • Medium-range order: examine the persistence or damping of RDF features beyond the first coordination shell.
  • Thermal stability: assess energy and temperature traces before selecting frames for structural averaging.

Limitations and next steps

  1. Verify the completion and continuity of each QE stage.
  2. Validate the final cell and structure, including any periodic-boundary and density assumptions.
  3. Choose and justify neighbour cutoffs before calculating coordination and bond statistics.
  4. Sample enough equilibrated frames to avoid interpreting one snapshot as a statistical ensemble.
  5. Where feasible, compare first-principles and classical results only after matching composition, thermal protocol, and analysis definitions.
