Sulfur and Selenium Incorporation on MoO₃(010)

Exploratory first-principles investigation of sulfur- and selenium-containing species near a MoO₃ surface, including candidate configurations and possible reaction pathways.

  • Surface modeling
  • S₂ / Se₂

01 / Overview

Research question

How might sulfur- and selenium-containing species interact with a MoO₃(010) surface, and what atomic rearrangements could occur along candidate incorporation pathways?

Project scope

Exploratory first-principles investigation of sulfur- and selenium-containing species near a MoO₃ surface, including candidate configurations and possible reaction pathways.

02 / Methods

Materials, methods & workflow

Surface-slab modeling, geometry relaxation of candidate adsorbed/initial/final configurations, and exploratory NEB pathway development using Quantum ESPRESSO.
